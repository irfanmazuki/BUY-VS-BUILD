Summary of Buy vs Build Pain Points

1. Slow and Heavy Process

The Buy vs Build process is too complianceheavy and takes too long.

It can take around 20 days just to decide, causing delays for the business to move to the next step.

Shape

2. Weak Cost & TCO Analysis

TCO calculations are done at a very high level and are not published or reusable.

Subscription and licensing costs are unclear.

There is no single, trusted source of existing application costs.

Shape

3. Poor Visibility of Existing Solutions

Limited information on existing applications used by other teams.

No known or clearly identified applications that can be reused.

Crosschecking with other domains is manual and timeconsuming.

Shape

4. EarlyStage Decisions Are Too Heavy

Assessments are too detailed for earlystage decisions.

Business expects Buy vs Build decisions to be quick, but the process is not lightweight.

Shape

5. Unclear Ownership and Decision Making

Too many stakeholders involved.

No single decision owner, leading to slow and unclear outcomes.

Shape

6. Lack of Reuse and Learning

No common repository for requirements, pain points, or lessons learned.

Past implementation experiences and user adoption data are not reused.

Shape

7. Weak Business Case and Planning

Business cases are often unclear.

Product roadmaps are created without firm budget commitment beyond the first year (e.g. no clear 3–5 year funding view).

Summary of Proposed Solutions

1. Improve Visibility of Existing Solutions

Create and maintain a catalogue of technical applications, including subscription fees, ownership, and whether they are vendor or internally developed.

Maintain a list of existing solutions from the current environment that can potentially be reused.

Shape

2. Strengthen EarlyStage Requirements

Define highlevel requirements upfront to guide Buy vs Build decisions without overengineering.

Ensure requirements are clear enough to support quick early decisions.

Shape

3. Centralise Market & Vendor Intelligence

Maintain a summary of market solutions based on sources such as Gartner, Forrester, and web research.

Use this as a reference point to quickly identify available Buy options.

Shape

4. Integrate Business and Technical View

Integrate technical and nontechnical applications/systems into a single view.

Clearly show key features and capabilities to support comparison and reuse decisions.
